How a project runs.

Eleven clear steps from first message to final files, so you always know where your project stands.

Start with a brief
  1. 01

    Inquiry & brief

    We discuss your requirements to understand your vision and goals.

  2. 02

    Scope & agreement

    Deliverables, timeline and pricing are set out clearly in a proposal.

  3. 03

    Deposit

    An invoice is sent for the agreed deposit. Work starts once payment is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Research

    We study industry trends, competitors and design inspiration.

  5. 05

    Initial concepts

    Two or three design options based on your brief and the research.

  6. 06

    Your feedback

    We present the concepts and gather detailed feedback.

  7. 07

    Refine & finalize

    Revisions on the chosen direction, then the final design is prepared.

  8. 08

    Approval

    You review the final design and sign off when you’re happy.

  9. 09

    Final payment

    An invoice for the remaining balance is sent before delivery.

  10. 10

    Delivery

    Final files are delivered securely and neatly organized.

  11. 11

    Follow-up

    Ongoing support whenever you need it, and a chance to share your feedback.
