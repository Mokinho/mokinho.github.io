Our story

Visuals that elevate your brand

MokPixel specialises in creating stunning visuals that elevate your brand. From our studio in Kuala Lumpur, we craft captivating logos, eye-catching mockups and impactful posters.

We understand that behind every brief is a business with real goals: a launch, an event, a campaign, a fresh start. So we take the time to understand your vision and translate it into designs that resonate with your audience.

Whether you’re a startup finding your identity or an established business ready for a refresh, we help you stand out in the market.
